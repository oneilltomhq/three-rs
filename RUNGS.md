## sdf-text scout (handoff/scouts/sdf-text/PLAN.md, 2026-09-13)

Two headline findings: three r186's NodeMaterial.setupPosition applies positionNode *after* the instance transform, which is the bug d33 worked around by baking glyph quads into instance matrices; the port implements position_node correctly (before the instance matrix) and still pixel-matches because the two are identical on a unit plane. The EDT binarises at alpha >= 128, so the Canvas-AA question shrinks to "which texels are >= 50% covered". Ladder: 1 font metrics/outlines vs opentype.js goldens, 2 raster+EDT+atlas tiles, 3 layout_text_vector + six lib3 layers, 4 renderer capabilities (instanced vertex attributes, R32Float + FLOAT32_FILTERABLE, fwidth/abs, position_node, per-fragment alpha, blending, transparent list), 5 BatchedText block, 6 d33 treemap labels. Steps 1–3 need no GPU; step 4's blending/transparent list is shared with rung 9.
